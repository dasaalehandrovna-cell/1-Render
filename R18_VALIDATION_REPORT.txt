LIMITATION
----------
The local validation environment cannot reproduce Render's exact live cut-over timing or Telegram network RTT. R18 therefore includes two independent deploy bridges (preboot old-front capture and pre-MEGA SIGTERM handoff) plus Worker/Redis freshness arbitration so correctness does not depend on one specific Render shutdown order.
